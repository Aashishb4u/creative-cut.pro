Features

The parts of clipping that eat your week.

Scored by two separate judges

One AI reads only what was said. Another sees only how loud the streamer got and how busy chat was. During a live stream, a clip is made when both agree, so you get moments, not filler.

FAQ

Got any questions left?

The things people ask before they run their first stream through Creative Cut.

Is Creative Cut free?

Yes. Creative Cut is free and open source. You run it on your own computer or server, and there are no plans or fees.

Which AI does it use?

Your own. Add an API key for Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Claude or OpenRouter, or point it at a local model. Whatever the AI costs is between you and that provider.

What happens without an AI key?

Creative Cut still makes clips, picking the loudest moments that have speech in them. Add a key and the AI chooses the moments and writes the hooks and titles.

Which videos work best?

Anything with people talking and reacting: live streams, gaming VODs, podcasts, interviews. Links can be from YouTube, Twitch or Kick. Captions are written from English speech.

How long are the clips, and what shape?

Vertical 9:16, the shape Shorts, Reels and TikTok use. They run 15 to 60 seconds by default and you can set your own range. Choose a blurred fill, a zoomed frame or a centre crop.

Can I change a clip afterwards?

Yes. Open any clip to trim the start and end, rewrite the hook and title, or switch caption style and layout, then re-make it.

Can I clip any streamer?

Creative Cut is a tool. You are responsible for having the right to clip and post a creator's content; most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
